# 🪞 Life Choice Guide

**English** | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

> **Stop looping on a hard choice.**  
> Strips away illusions about how reality works, puts your actual leverage on the table, and exposes the unspoken desires, fears, and excuses keeping you stuck.

---

## 🎯 What It Does

When you're torn, procrastinating, or want a brutal stress-test on a decision you've already made, this skill walks through four clear lenses in one go:

- **🌍 External Reality**: How this game actually works, what winners really paid, and where naive plans fail.
- **🧭 Your Real Position**: What chips you actually hold (cash, skills, time, fallback) and what each path costs you.
- **🪞 Blunt Self-Reflection**: A first-person mirror that strips away polite excuses to name the exact desire or fear holding you back.
- **✅ Clear Verdict**: No fence-sitting — compares the **Comfort Path** (avoiding real cost) vs. the **Cost Path** (paying the right price) and gives one actionable recommendation.

---

## ⚠️ Read Before Using (High-Sharpness Warning)

This skill is intentionally sharp. Decide for yourself whether it fits your current state before using it:

1. **Not an emotional comfort bot or therapy** — It will not sugarcoat, validate excuses, or tell you *"you're already doing great."* Its entire value is blunt clarity.
2. **Expect discomfort** — The self-reflection section writes in the first person (*"I"*) to call out unflattering fears and rationalizations. If you want warmth, venting, or reassurance right now, **skip this skill**.
3. **Know its limits** — The less context you share, the more it has to ask or infer. It is a structured thinking tool for everyday career, business, and personal choices, **never a substitute for medical, mental health, legal, or financial professionals**.

| ✅ Use This When | ❌ Do NOT Use This When |
| --- | --- |
| You're stuck in hesitation or procrastination and want an honest wake-up call | You feel fragile and mainly want empathy, comfort, or someone to listen |
| You're facing a career, business, or relationship crossroads and want cold reality first | You are in acute emotional crisis, severe depression, or an unsafe situation |
| You've already decided on a plan and want a strict stress-test for blind spots | You only want praise or confirmation for what you already picked |

## 💬 How to Get the Best Results

Write to your Agent just like an unfiltered diary entry. To get the sharpest, most accurate breakdown, include three things:

1. **What actually happened** (concrete events, not just *"I feel lost"*);
2. **Your real constraints & chips** (savings/runway, skills, time, family or financial obligations);
3. **What you're torn between — or the decision you already made**.

### Example Prompts

- *"I've been stalling on quitting my job to build a SaaS for 6 months. Here's my savings, skills, and what happened this week — tear my excuses apart and tell me what to do."*
- *"I already signed up to invest $20K into a friend's café. Stress-test this decision and tell me where the fatal traps are."*
